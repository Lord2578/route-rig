RouteRig — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

RouteRig is a truck-aware route planning app. This page explains what data the app uses and why. RouteRig is an independent portfolio project, not a commercial product — there is no account system and no data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Location data

RouteRig uses your device's location for two purposes:

  • While using the app (foreground): to set your current position as the default starting point for a route and to show it on the map.
  • In the background (optional): if you grant "Always" location access, RouteRig keeps tracking your position while the app is backgrounded so it can send you a local notification when you're getting close to your destination. This only happens after a route has been planned, and only until you close the app or the route changes. You can decline this and still use every other feature of the app.

Location data is processed on your device and sent to OpenRouteService (see below) to calculate routes. RouteRig does not store your location history or send it to any server of its own.

Address search and routing (OpenRouteService)

When you search for an address or plan a route, the text you type and the coordinates involved are sent to OpenRouteService, a third-party routing API, in order to return search results and directions. See OpenRouteService's own privacy policy for how they handle that data.

Data stored on your device

Saved routes and your truck's dimensions (height, weight, length) are stored locally on your device only, using standard app storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What RouteRig does not do

  • No account creation, login, or personal profile
  • No analytics, ad tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties for marketing
